# Assign or Talk to a Client Owner

If Dela suggests assigning or contacting a client owner, you can do so from the Pursuit Score panel.

A dedicated client owner is responsible for maintaining the relationship with the client over time. That ongoing relationship gives you better visibility into the client's business, priorities, and reliability, which feeds directly into a more informed go/no-go decision.

<span class="note__title">Note:</span> A different name is displayed if the **Owner** field has been modified in the Screen Designer.</div>
 
**To assign or talk to a firm owner:**

1. On the left side of the project record, click the **Pursuit Score value** to open the Pursuit Score panel.
2. In the Pursuit Score panel, scroll to the Suggestions section. 
3. Click **Assign client owner**. An information bubble appears. <span class="note__title">Note:</span> If a client owner is already assigned, click **Talk to client owner about client relationship** instead.</div>
4. In the information bubble, click **More...** to open the associated firm record. <span class="note__title">Note:</span> If you don't have access to the firm's record, the information bubble displays the client's information instead.</div>
5. On the firm record, assign a client owner or communicate with the current client owner using the contact information provided.
