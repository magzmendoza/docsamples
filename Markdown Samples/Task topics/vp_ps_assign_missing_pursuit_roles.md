# Assign Missing Pursuit Roles

If Dela suggests assigning missing pursuit roles, you can do so from the Pursuit Score panel.

This suggestion appears when a pursuit role is unassigned. Having a dedicated person responsible for monitoring and following up on the pursuit is key to staying on top of it and maintaining good intel from the client. Without clear ownership, important signals can be missed, and the go/no-go decision may be based on outdated or incomplete information.
 
**To assign missing pursuit roles:**

1. On the left side of the project record, click the **Pursuit Score value** to open the Pursuit Score panel.
2. In the Pursuit Score panel, scroll to the Suggestions section. 
3. Click the link in the **Assign Missing Pursuit Role/s**. You are redirected to the Overview tab of the project record.
4. Fill up the missing field roles in the Overview tab.
