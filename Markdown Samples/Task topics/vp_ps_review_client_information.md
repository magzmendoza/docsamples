# Review Client Information

If Dela suggests reviewing client information, you can do so from the Pursuit Score panel.

This suggestion appears when a client-based factor is pulling the score down. Common causes include low profitability on past projects with this client, a limited project history together, or a low win/loss ratio. Reviewing the client record helps you understand what's driving the score and decide whether the pursuit is worth the investment.
 
**To review firm information:**

1. On the left side of the project record, click the **Pursuit Score value** to open the Pursuit Score panel.
2. In the Pursuit Score panel, scroll to the Suggestions section. 
3. Click **Review Client Information**. You are redirected to the associated client's record.

    <span class="note__title">Note:</span> If you don't have access to the client's record, an information bubble displays the client's information instead.</div>
