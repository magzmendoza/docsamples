# Complete Missing Scoring Fields

If Dela suggests completing missing scoring fields, you can do so from the Pursuit Score panel.

The Pursuit Score helps you decide whether to pursue a project. If a scoring field is missing information (or set to 0 for numeric fields), the score can't fully reflect the strength of your position, and pursuing a project without good intel is itself a risk.

Complete these fields as soon as the information is known so the score helps inform a good go/no-go decision.
 
**To complete missing scoring fields:**

1. On the left side of the project record, click the **Pursuit Score value** to open the Pursuit Score panel.
2. In the Pursuit Score panel, scroll to the Suggestions section. 
3. Click the link in the **Complete missing scoring field** suggestion. You are redirected to the section in the project record where the missing scoring field or fields are located.
