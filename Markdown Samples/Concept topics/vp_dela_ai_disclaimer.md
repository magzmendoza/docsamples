# Dela AI Disclaimer

Insight Dela panels now include an information icon to the right of the panel title. The icon provides important information about AI-generated content in {{vars.vantagepoint}}. The disclaimer applies consistently to on-demand Dela features, including Earned Value Analysis, Project Smart Summary, Client Smart Summary, Insights, and Pursuit Score.

## Viewing the Dela AI Disclaimer

Hover over the **information icon** to display the Disclaimer information bubble. The disclaimer states:

*{{vars.vantagepoint}} uses artificial intelligence to generate content from your own {{vars.vantagepoint}} data and is subject to the {{vars.vantagepoint}} Product Terms. AI-generated outputs do not constitute representations or advice by Deltek, and Deltek does not verify their accuracy or completeness. Users are responsible for reviewing and validating such information before use. To report inaccurate or unexpected outputs, contact the Deltek Support Center.*
*Copyright [current year] Deltek, Inc.*
