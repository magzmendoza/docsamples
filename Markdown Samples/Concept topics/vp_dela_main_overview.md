# Dela

Dela is Deltek's artificial intelligence (AI) platform for Vantagepoint. It helps users find information, gain insights, summarize business data, and perform tasks using natural language.

Dela brings together AI-powered experiences across Vantagepoint to help firms work more efficiently, make informed decisions, and reduce the time spent searching for information or completing routine activities.

Depending on your Vantagepoint deployment model, the Dela capabilities available to your organization may vary.


## How Dela Helps You

Dela uses your organization's Vantagepoint data to provide insights, recommendations, and assistance across business workflows.

With Dela, users can:

- Ask questions using natural language
- Generate summaries of firms, projects, and other business records
- Identify trends, risks, and opportunities
- Receive AI-generated insights to support decision-making
- Perform supported business actions through conversational interactions

## Key Benefits of Dela

Dela helps organizations:

- Improve productivity through conversational AI experiences
- Reduce the time required to find information
- Gain faster access to business insights and trends
- Support data-driven decision-making
- Simplify complex workflows
- Increase visibility into business performance

## Security and Access

Dela respects Vantagepoint security settings and user permissions. Users can access only the data and functionality available to them based on their assigned security roles and permissions.
