# Dela Deployment Options

Dela capabilities vary based on your {{vars.vantagepoint}} deployment model. Beginning with {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.4, Deltek offers a rearchitected and enhanced version of Dela that is available exclusively to Vantagepoint {{vars.delacloud}} (SaaS) customers.

Customers using an {{vars.onpremises}} deployment continue to have access to the Dela capabilities that are available through {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.3. However, the new rearchitected Dela introduced in {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.4 is only available to {{vars.vantagepoint}} {{vars.delacloud}} customers. 

## Access Enhanced Dela Features in {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.4 and Later

To use the enhanced Dela features in {{vars.vantagepoint}} {{vars.delacloud}}, administrators must opt in to **Dela: Dela Expansion** in **Settings > General > Opt-in Features**. The opt-in is available starting with the Vantagepoint 2026.4 release and is temporary. In a future release, Deltek will enable the feature automatically and remove the opt-in requirement. For more information about opt-in features, see [Opt-In Features Overview](/topics/Settings%20and%20Configuration/General%20Settings/vp_set_optin.md).

## Dela Feature Availability

This table provides a quick reference to help you identify feature support across Cloud and On-premises environments, enabling you to plan adoption and implementation accordingly. 

| Feature Area|{{vars.onpremises}}|{{vars.delacloud}}|
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask Dela | Yes | Yes |
| Dela Expansion | No | Yes |
| On-Demand Insights | No | Yes (Opt-In Available as part of Dela Expansion) |
| Customer Intelligence Agent | No | Yes (Opt-In Available as part of Dela Expansion) |
| Pursuit Score | No | Yes (Opt-In Available as part of Dela Expansion) |
| Smart Summaries | Yes | Yes |
| System-Generated Insights | Yes | Yes |

For questions about Dela, please refer to [Dela for Vantagepoint FAQs](https://learning.deltek.com/bundle/DelaforVantagepointFAQs/page/Dela_for_Vantagepoint_FAQs.html).

## Supported Domains for Dela Expansion in {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.4 and Later

Learn which business domains Dela Expansion supports in {{vars.vantagepoint}} 2026.4 and the actions available within each domain.

| Domain|Scope|
| --- | --- | 
| Absence Requests | Submit and manage time-off requests, check approval status, and look up available time-off balances: Vacation, Sick, PTO, and other accrual types.|
| Accounts Receivable | Look up account receivable balances, invoice details, and payment or retainer activity for a client or project.|
| Activities | Create, update, and manage activities such as calls, meetings, and follow-ups, and link them to the right firms, projects, contacts, or employees.| 
| Boilerplates | Find, create, and update standard proposal text: boilerplate language, resumes, and qualification statements, including any attached files.|
| Contacts | Look up, create, and update contact records, including lead and qualification status, and see which marketing campaigns a contact is linked to.|
| Employees | Look up, create, and update employee information: job details, credentials, licenses, skills, education, and project history.|
| Expense Report | Create and submit expense reports and review the status and details of existing reports.|
| Files | Attach files to a record, or retrieve files already attached, across any supported record type.|
| Firms | Look up, create, and update firm records: clients, vendors, subcontractors, and prospects — including firm relationships and related documents.|
| Lists | Look up the meaning of coded values used throughout Vantagepoint, so codes show up as readable labels instead of raw codes.|
| Marketing Campaigns | Create and manage marketing campaigns, including contacts, firms, and projects tied to a campaign, and campaign responses. |
| Organizations  | Look up organizational information, such as which office or business unit a record belongs to. |
| Projects  | Look up, create, and update project records — from pursuit through completion — including project details, team members, contracts, and staffing plans. Get project financial summaries such as revenue, cost, and profitability.|
| Pursuit Score  | Review and adjust how pursuit opportunities are scored, including the underlying scoring factors and weights. |
| User Defined  | Work with any custom hub your organization has configured — create, update, and attach files to custom records |
| Navigation  | Retrieve a link to a specific record. Use the link to jump straight to an employee, project, or other record instead of searching for it.|
