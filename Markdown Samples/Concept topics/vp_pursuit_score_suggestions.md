# Pursuit Score Suggestions

Pursuit Score Suggestions are AI-generated recommendations that appear in the Pursuit Score panel after a pursuit score is generated. These suggestions help users improve pursuit readiness, address data gaps, and take meaningful actions based on the factors that influence a project's pursuit score. Rather than only presenting a score, the Pursuit Score panel provides actionable guidance that helps teams make informed go/no-go decisions and strengthen pursuit planning.  

The Pursuit Scoring Assistant analyzes available project, client, and pursuit information to identify opportunities for improving data quality, supporting decision-making, and recommend actions based on the available information.

Pursuit Score Suggestions are displayed in the Suggestions section of the Pursuit Score panel and vary depending on the specific conditions identified during score evaluation. Recommendations appear only when relevant to the selected pursuit. For example, suggestions may be shown when important scoring fields are incomplete, key pursuit roles are unassigned, or additional client context could help evaluate the opportunity. 

The following Pursuit Score Suggestions are available: 

- **Complete Missing Scoring Fields**: Identifies missing information that may affect the pursuit score.  
- **Review Client Information**: Encourages users to review firm-related information and relationship data.  
- **Assign Missing Pursuit Roles**: Recommends assigning key pursuit team members when required roles are unassigned.  
- **Add activity for check-in**: Helps users record follow-up actions such as meetings or check-ins.  
- **Assign/talk to the Client Owner**: Encourages collaboration with the Primary Client Owner or Account Manager.  
- **Add Quick Notes (Optional)**: Allows users to document insights or context related to the pursuit without creating a follow-up activity.  

<span class="note__title">Note:</span> Pursuit Score suggestions are available only after a pursuit score has been generated. The receommendations available depend on the information associated with the selected pursuit and may differ from one project to another.</div>

## How to Access Pursuit Score Suggestions

To access pursuit score suggestions in {{vars.vantagepoint}} {{vars.delacloud}}, administrators must opt in to **Dela: Dela Expansion** in **Settings > General > Opt-in Features**. The **Dela Expansion** opt-in is needed to enable the Pursuit Score feature. The opt-in is available starting with the Vantagepoint 2026.4 release and is temporary. In a future release, Deltek will enable the feature automatically and remove the opt-in requirement. For more information about opt-in features, see [Opt-In Features Overview](/topics/Settings%20and%20Configuration/General%20Settings/vp_set_optin.md).

For more information, please refer to [Enable and Configure Pursuit Score](/topics/Settings%20and%20Configuration/Dela%20Settings/vp_settings_dela_enable_pursuit_score.md).
