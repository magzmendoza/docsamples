# Pursuit Score Panel

The Pursuit Score panel evaluates in-pursuit projects using an AI-driven scoring model, highlights key drivers and potential risks, and provides recommendations to support go/no-go decisions and improve pursuit outcomes.

[[INCLUDE:conrefs/Conref_Dela.md#conref_delanewfeature]]

The Pursuit Score panel includes:

- **Pursuit Score**: Displays the overall AI-generated score for the selected pursuit based on factors defined by your administrator.
- **Score Factors**: Displays the key factors that contributed to the generated Pursuit Score and may identify areas of strength or potential risk.
- **Actionable Suggestions**: Displays recommendations that help improve pursuit readiness or data quality. Available suggestions may include creating activities, reviewing client information, assigning team members, completing project data, consulting a firm owner, or adding quick notes.

## Where to Find Your Pursuit Score Panel

Pursuit Score panels appear as on-demand panels in the new {{vars.vantagepoint}} user experience when you generate Pursuit Score on a project record. The panel displays the Pursuit Score generated with actionable suggestions which allows you to collapse or close as needed.
