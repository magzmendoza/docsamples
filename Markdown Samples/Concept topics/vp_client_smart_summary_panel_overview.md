# Client Smart Summary Panel

The Client Smart Summary panel provides an AI-generated overview of client information, including recent activities, active and completed projects, top in-pursuit projects, and key performance indicators (KPIs), to help you quickly understand client relationships and performance.

\{\{INCLUDE:conrefs/Use the Smart Summaries Logo Paragraph and Sections.md\#P\_ED88C3EE16EE44DA9A14DF9D8523B36F\}\}

## Where to Find Your Client Smart Summary Panel

Client Smart Summary panels appear as on-demand panels in the new Vantagepoint user experience when you generate a Smart Summary from the **Dela Actions** button on a firm record. The panel displays the generated Client Smart Summary and allows you to collapse or close as needed.
