# How to Work with Pursuit Score Suggestions (Opt-In Available)

Pursuit Score suggestions are AI-generated recommendations that appear within the Pursuit Score panel. These suggestions help you take action on insights generated from a projects, clients, and pursuit data without leaving the context of your pursuit evaluation.

Rather than simply displaying a score, the Pursuit Score panel provides actionable guidance that helps users make informed decisions and improve the quality of project and client data. By acting on these recommendations, teams can better evaluate opportunities and increase confidence in pursuit planning and strategy.
