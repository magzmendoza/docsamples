# Options on the Ask Dela Tab Panel

Use the options on the Ask Dela tab panel to manage your questions or commands.

## Contents

<!-- FIELD-TABLE-START -->
-   **Ask Dela Box**

    Enter your questions or commands in the Ask Dela box, then click the **Send** icon or press the ENTER key to submit. You can also use the **Speech to Text** icon to dictate your inquiries.

-   **Thumbs up**

    Click this icon if you are satisfied with Ask Dela's response.

-   **Thumbs down**

    Click this icon if you are not satisfied with Ask Dela's response.

-   **Email**

    Click this icon to open the Email dialog box. You can send an email to a specific employee if you want more information about your inquiry.

-   **Copy**

    Click this icon to copy a response.
